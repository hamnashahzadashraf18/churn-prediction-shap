Problem Statement
Churn means a customer leaves the company. Winning a new customer usually costs far more than keeping an existing one, so predicting churn early lets a business act before it loses revenue.
This project answers two questions:
Who is likely to churn? (prediction)
Why are they likely to churn? (explainability)
